About Parth Enterprise

Trusted Exporter with Years of Industry Experience

Rooted in Quality, Driven by Trust

Established in 2016, Parth Enterprise has grown into a leading B2B exporter of premium agricultural commodities. Operating from Patan, Gujarat, we have built a legacy on uncompromising quality and ethical business practices.

With over a decade of core agricultural experience and specialized direct export operations spanning the last 3+ years, our team understands the exact specifications required by global markets.

From the fields to the shipping ports, our comprehensive multi-tier sorting, machine cleaning, and quality testing ensure that our buyers receive only the absolute best grade of Bajra, Millet, and Wheat.
