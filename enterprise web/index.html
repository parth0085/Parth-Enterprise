Who We Are

Established in 2016, Parth Enterprise is a premier B2B agricultural export company based in Patan, Gujarat. We specialize in sourcing and delivering the highest grade of Bajra, Millet, and Wheat to international markets.

Our commitment to uncompromising quality and transparent business practices has earned us the trust of buyers worldwide. Every grain we export passes through rigorous quality checks to meet global standards.
